Hi kids! Today I want to tell you about the story of a brilliant schizophrenic named Terry Davis.  <br>
<br>
A short disclaimer, I do not condone most of his thoughts but I am fascinated by his awareness of his condition and his first principle thinking. This is just the tip of the iceberg
of some stuff I found inspiring, viewer discretion is advised if you decide to dig deeper into his life. <br>
<br>
All you need to know about him is that he was a programmer, that after stopping to work developed for 10 years his own operating system (similar to the commodor64 that he loved) made from scratch with a low level programming language he created as a mix between C and C++ (perhaps C+... his words) that he called "Holy C" with its own 64bit compiler obviously. Completely for fun, nothing more.

<div style="max-width: 600px; margin: 20px auto; padding: 20px; border: 2px solid #333; border-radius: 10px; text-align: center; box-shadow: 0 4px 8px rgba(0,0,0,0.1);">
<b>"TempleOS is for programming as entreteinement. It empowers programmers with kernel privilege because it's fun. It allows full access to everything because it's fun. It has not beurocracy because it's fun. It is the way it is by choice because it's fun. TempleOS is by no way a Windows or Linux wannabe -- that would be pointless. It is not trying to win a prize for low resources usage, low line count is a goal though... I'm an expert of knowing what is fun about programming, if you are so smart tell me what's fun and not fun about programming"</b>
</div>

He lived with his parents off of his social security checks, he got hospitalizations multiple times and he was diagnesed as schizophrenic. He then got kicked out for not taking his prescribed meds and started living homeless in his van. All while streaming and recording. In full isolation from any physical human interaction.
He died in 2018 after being hit by a train. <br>
